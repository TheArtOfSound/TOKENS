# TOKENS — Product Requirements (Phase 1)

Derived from the dossier. Requirements are marked: ✅ met · ◑ partial · ⬜ future.

## Product thesis
A **local-first AI-activity ledger and verified AI-work network**. The local collector is the evidence infrastructure; the site is the professional interpretation layer. Raw token volume is never represented as intelligence, expertise, productivity, or employability by itself.

The causal chain must stay explicit at every step:
`local AI activity → normalized evidence → privacy-preserving aggregate → professional identity → discoverability → paid opportunity → verified outcome`.

## Principles (enforced now)
- ✅ **Local-first:** meaningful analytics with no account and no cloud sync.
- ✅ **Evidence classes, not vague verification:** every metric carries a source class + method.
- ✅ **No universal score:** dimensions shown separately with methods; no opaque composite.
- ✅ **Content minimization:** raw prompts/responses/code/paths never persisted or published.
- ✅ **Open, inspectable collector:** source + public schema + methods documented.
- ◑ **Explicit, revocable consent:** privacy posture + `fieldsPublished` shown; full interactive upload-preview with per-field toggles = roadmap.
- ✅ **Promotion integrity (design):** data model keeps organic evidence separate from any future paid placement.

## Users & jobs-to-be-done
- ✅ **AI power user:** one accurate view across Claude Code + Codex with lifetime history, daily patterns, cost, cache effectiveness, and a shareable surface. (Gemini/Cursor/Ollama/etc. = future adapters.)
- ⬜ **AI-native builder:** connect repos/deployments/research/case studies with validated outcomes (future profile graph).
- ⬜ **Paid evaluator / research participant:** eligible opportunities with clear compensation + privacy (future marketplace).
- ⬜ **Recruiter / hiring manager:** filter by verified activity + evidence explanations, no vanity leaderboard (future).
- ⬜ **AI company / research team:** verified cohorts for evaluations/beta/interviews (future).
- ⬜ **Enterprise leader:** intentionally deferred until privacy/governance mature.

## Verification categories (labels the platform may use)
`collector verified` · `provider reported` · `sustained usage` · `high-volume activity` · `active across multiple projects` · `identity verified` · `work verified` · `outcome verified` · `assessment verified`. **Never** infer "expert" from usage volume.

## Profile requirements (future phase, data model designed for)
Lead with professional identity, not a token number. Structures for: name/pseudonym, headline, location, bio, availability, work categories, tools/models used, active AI-work days, measured sessions, verified token categories, usage history, connected repos, deployed products, publications, case studies, evaluations, client/employer-confirmed outcomes, external links, contact prefs, verification status.

## Website experience (Phase-1 subset shipped)
- ✅ Landing + product explanation + privacy hooks.
- ✅ Personal/observatory dashboard from real normalized data.
- ✅ Methodology & confidence explanations (measurement classes).
- ✅ Supported tools (Claude Code, Codex) + provider/model split.
- ✅ Verification/proof panel with honest claims.
- ⬜ Onboarding/installation flow, account & privacy controls UI, public professional profile, opportunity preferences — future phases.

## Non-goals (now)
Marketplace, employer search, messaging, social feed, universal score, fabricated activity/logos/testimonials, enterprise surveillance. Sample/demo data must be prominently labeled (the snapshot carries `isSampleData` + `source`).
